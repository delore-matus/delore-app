DeLore

DeLore lets people rent out idle GPU capacity to run AI compute jobs for others. Providers are paid automatically per completed job, and the platform takes a transparent fee. The service is currently in testnet/demo mode, and no real payouts are made. Use of the service is governed by Terms and Conditions.
